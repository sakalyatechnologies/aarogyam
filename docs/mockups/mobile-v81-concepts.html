Rule. No patient name and no vitals on the island or the lock screen. Patient data must never show on a lock screen, so the island carries only chair, token and time.

C2Horseshoe arch and thumb rail

  1. Tap C2: opens Meera Shah's visit.
  2. Drag the arch to tilt it. Tilt and Flat view do the same.
  3. Tap a tooth (or use the arrows), then pick a surface: M, D, O, B or L.
  4. The diagnosis sheet opens; choose one and Add to chart.
  5. Bottom right: press and slide to an action, or tap the green button then tap an action.
Gesture, then button. Drag to tilt = Tilt buttons. Slide from the corner = tap, then tap. Tap a tooth = arrows. Surface ring = surface buttons.

C3Shared-element bento sheets

  1. Tap C3: Today scrolls to the tiles.
  2. Tap Chairs, Collected or Lab cases: the tile grows into a sheet and shrinks back on close.
  3. Drag the Up next carousel past its end: it stretches and springs back. The arrow buttons do the same.
  4. Preview as Assistant (top bar): the money tile disappears.

C4Voice whisper capsule

  1. Tap C4: opens the visit at the voice card.
  2. Tap the emerald pill. Words appear; tooth, diagnosis and drug are picked out into chips.
  3. Tap ✓ to add one, ✕ to dismiss. The counters stay at 0 until you do.
  4. Try EN, हिं and मरा. Meera has a penicillin allergy: adding amoxicillin raises a warning.
Rule. Suggestions only. Nothing reaches the notes, chart or Rx until the doctor taps ✓.

C5Doctor ⇄ Front desk

  1. Tap C5: opens the layout switch from Today.
  2. Flip between Doctor (operatory, odontogram, Rx, money hidden) and Front desk (intake, photo, consent, UPI QR).
Rule. The switch is shown only to members who hold both roles. It changes layout only; permissions still come from the server.
