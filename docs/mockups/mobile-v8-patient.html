Aarogyam v8 · Patient and visit

v7 with a simpler patient page (Overview, History, Consent) and one Start treatment screen: timer, dictated or typed notes, and a prescription that opens in place with morning, afternoon and night doses. Sample patients only.

iPhone · Liquid Glass
Android · Material 3 Expressive
Try this. Sign in, then Patients → Meera Shah. Tap Visits, paid and due in the header to show the money. Overview has the teeth, the treatment list and X-rays; History has one card per visit, and each opens with its notes, prescription and bill. Consent lists what the patient agreed to.
Start treatment. The timer runs from when the patient sat down. Dictate (English, Hindi or Marathi) or Write a point; tap a tag to change Complaint, Finding or Advice. Open Write prescription: add Amoxicillin to see the allergy swap, set morning, afternoon and night with − and +, pick before or after food and the days, then Sign and issue.
Close visit. If the prescription isn't signed it asks first. The summary shows the bill; Back returns to History with the new visit on top. Switch to Assistant to see money hidden and no Start treatment.
