Aarogyam is the clinic app for doctors and their teams. Doctors, front desk, assistants and owners each sign in and see only what their role allows.

Front desk
• Find a patient by mobile number, or register a new one with consent and family.
• See who is waiting, who is with the doctor and who still has to pay.
• Collect payment by UPI, cash or card, with a photo of the proof.

Doctors
• Your waiting list, with a reminder when a patient has been waiting too long.
• Start the visit, write or dictate notes, and issue a prescription with morning, afternoon and night doses, with an allergy check as you add medicines.
• Send the patient a clear visit summary on WhatsApp.
• Specialty tools, starting with dental charting of teeth and surfaces.
• Wrap up with the follow-up and fee in one step; the front desk is told to collect.

The whole team
• Today at a glance, tasks with photo proof, lab cases from order to delivery, and team chat.
• English, Hindi and Marathi.

Your clinic's data stays with your clinic. The app needs an Aarogyam clinic account: ask your clinic owner for an invitation.
