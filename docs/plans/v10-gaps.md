# v10 gaps for the web and backend agent

Found on 11 Oct 2026 while planning the assistant and doctor phone journeys ([v10-staff-journeys.md](v10-staff-journeys.md)). The phone works around each one, so none of them blocks mobile. Checked against `origin/main` deb08ed8 `docs/api/openapi.json`.

| # | Gap | Why it matters | Suggested shape | Phone fallback until then |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | **No way to re-notify a doctor** when a queued patient isn't called within about 60 s | The founder wants the desk to nudge, or go and check | `POST /queue/{id}/remind`, permission `queue.write`, rate-limited (one per 60 s per token). It writes another `patient_waiting` (or `patient_waiting_reminder`) staff notification for that practitioner | display-only amber "Check with Dr X" after 60 s; Remind is hidden until this lands (no app-side nudge, per the single-backend rule) |
| G2 | **No education pages** (ask: put placeholder `/learn` links in the welcome template now, before the pages exist) (hygiene, treatment explainers, animated videos) on the clinic site to link from the welcome message | BE9; the founder wants a link in the welcome WhatsApp | public `/<clinic>/learn` with placeholder cards and video slots, linked from the `welcome` template | the welcome message carries the app link and wait time only |
| G3 | **No push notifications** (FCM / APNs) | Doctors and desk miss events while the app is in the background | provider adapter through the notification service; device tokens table; payloads carry ids only | SSE while open; refresh on foreground; in-app banners |
| G4 | **Task completion has no proof photo.** `CompleteBody` is `{client_id, note, usage}` | The founder wants "mark completed with proof" | `attachment_id` on `CompleteBody` and `TaskOccurrence`; a new attachment kind or label `task_proof`, not tied to a patient (needs a staff-scoped attachment owner) | note only; the proof photo is disabled with "Needs server support" |
| G5 | **The web desk has no family picker or payment-proof UI** (the backend comes from the v9 port: `GET/POST /patients/{id}/family`, lookup `family_group`, walk-in `family_of`, `Payment.attachment_id`) | The same flow on laptop and phone | add to `walk-in-wizard.tsx`, `add-to-queue.tsx` and `collect-dialog.tsx` | — (the phone has it) |
| G6 | **The walk-in has no "reason for visit" or initial note.** `WalkInRequest` has no note | The desk captures it; the doctor wants to see it first | `reason` (short text, at most 200 characters) on `WalkInRequest`, stored on the queue token or appointment and returned in `QueueToken` | the reason field is hidden until this lands (the backend agent has taken it) |
| G7 | **No patient profile photo pointer.** Photos are plain attachments | The desk takes the photo at registration; every screen should show it | `profile_attachment_id` on the patient (set when a `photo` attachment is labelled "Profile"); returned in `PatientBrief` | the newest attachment labelled "Profile" is fetched on the patient screen only |
| G8 | **No way for the desk to see that the welcome / thank-you message was sent or skipped** (no consent, no WhatsApp) | The founder wants the assistant to know the message went | `last_message: {key, status, skip_reason}` on `QueueToken`, or `GET /queue/{id}/messages` | no chip |
| G9 | **The thank-you message has to fire when the payment settles**, not only at finish (check pb's port: `visit_summary` trigger) | The founder asked for "on marking completion" | trigger on the payment that clears the visit's invoice, or on the token moving to `done` | — |
| G11 | **Patient-app visit list** `GET /me/patient/visits[/{id}]`: widen the 0261 patient_account RLS on encounters, procedures and recalls; add `app.visit_advice` (signed Plan section only). Reference code on branch v9-pa | the patient app | as in the reference | none, since it is not needed for the staff journeys |
| G10 | **The signature on the consent:** `POST /walk-ins` `consents` has no signature | The founder asked for a signed consent | `signature_attachment_id` on the consent record (kind `consent`) | upload as a `consent` attachment labelled "Signature" right after the walk-in |
| G12 | **Server-side overpayment guard:** reject a `POST /payments` allocation above the invoice balance when the desk and the doctor collect at the same time | Two people can collect at once | 409 `overpayment` with the current balance | the phone re-GETs the balance before paying |

The pa/pb port branches (`v9-pa`, `v9-pb`, then `feat/v9-port`) add the family, payment proof, SSE, auto-message, follow-up-link and recall-dismiss endpoints on top of main, using migrations 0700–0704. Please don't take those numbers.
