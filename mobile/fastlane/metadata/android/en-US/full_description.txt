Aarogyam is the clinic app for every doctor: general physicians, paediatricians, gynaecologists, dermatologists, dentists, ENT and eye specialists, physiotherapists and more. It works for a solo practice and for a multi-doctor, multi-specialty clinic. Doctors, front desk, assistants and owners each sign in and see only what their role allows.

For doctors
• Your waiting list, with a reminder when a patient has waited too long.
• Open any patient's history: past visits, notes, prescriptions, photos and bills.
• Write or dictate visit notes, and issue a prescription with morning, afternoon and night doses, with an allergy check as you add medicines.
• Send the patient a clear visit summary on WhatsApp.
• Specialty tools, starting with dental charting of teeth and surfaces.
• Wrap up with the follow-up and fee in one step; the front desk is told to collect.

For the front desk
• Find a patient by name or mobile number, or register a new one with consent and family.
• Check patients in, or book an appointment for later.
• See who is waiting, who is with the doctor and who still has to pay.
• Record payment by UPI, cash or card, with a photo of the proof.

For the whole team
• Today at a glance, tasks with photo proof, lab cases from order to delivery, and team chat.
• English, Hindi and Marathi.

Your clinic's data stays with your clinic. Sign in with your Aarogyam clinic account. Your clinic owner can invite you, or contact us through the support page to set up Aarogyam at your clinic.
